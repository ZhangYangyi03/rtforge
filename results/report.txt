L4 motion control: hand-written dynamics + feedforward
    measured  scenario                    ff=1.0    ff=0.0   ratio
              clean                        0.758    38.853   51.3x
              friction asymmetry 30%       0.551    38.711   70.3x
              backlash 0.05 deg          259.869   273.824    1.1x
              flex 2 Hz                   47.757    48.670    1.0x
              all three                  258.935   260.259    1.0x
    reading   full inverse-dynamics feedforward reduces worst-case tracking error 51x against a CLEAN plant, and essentially not at all against backdriveable real-world effects: 1.1x with backlash, 1.0x with structural flex.
    caveat    backlash magnitude 0.05 deg is a typical harmonic-drive figure; at 3.2 m/s tip speed it dominates because the arm reverses direction 60 times a second at this trajectory. A slower trajectory would show a much smaller number. The comparison across rows is what matters, not the absolute mm.
    measured  disturbance observer added on top -- and it does not work:
              backlash only        no DOB   259.87 mm   with DOB   259.88 mm
              flex only            no DOB    47.76 mm   with DOB    47.76 mm
              friction asymmetry   no DOB     0.55 mm   with DOB     2.48 mm
              all three            no DOB   258.94 mm   with DOB   259.94 mm
    reading   A disturbance observer estimates a TORQUE. Backlash is not a torque error: inside the deadband the output does not move no matter what torque is applied, so the observer sees a large mismatch, integrates it, and applies more torque into the gap -- which produces overshoot the instant the gap is crossed. The flex mode is a state the observer's model does not have, and the observer's low-pass is slower than the mode. Both therefore come out at exactly 1.00x.
    absent    C++ self-test binary not built here (cmake -S cpp -B cpp/build && cmake --build cpp/build); the dynamics CI job runs it
